About Rasmus Rosenberg Larsen

Rasmus Rosenberg Larsen is Associate Professor of Forensic Epistemology and Philosophy of Science at the University of Toronto Mississauga.

He is an Affiliated Scientist at the National Center for Ontological Research.

His work lies at the intersection of psychology, epistemology and law, and asks how science can improve decisions in the legal system.

His research appears in The Lancet Psychiatry; Psychology, Public Policy, and Law; and Philosophical Psychology.

His book Psychopathy Unmasked (2025) is published by the MIT Press.

He holds a PhD in philosophy of science from the University at Buffalo.

Expertise

Available for legal consultation, public talks and ontology work. Fees depend on the scope of the job; pro bono for organisations that advance public understanding of science.

Law and personality disorders

Guidance for legal teams on psychopathy and antisocial personality disorder, including how to respond to expert evidence that presents these labels as diagnoses with fixed prognoses.

Talks and lectures

Talks on science and society, forensic psychology and epistemology, tailored to courts, bar associations, universities and public audiences.

Ontology and taxonomy

Ontology development for structuring data in the health and cognitive sciences, grounded in Basic Formal Ontology and work on OBO Foundry projects.

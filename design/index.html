rasmusrosenberg.com: design mockups, rounds 1 to 3

Same content in six directions. Each has a light and dark mode (button in the header) and works at phone width.

  • A. FolioPaper texture, black ink, a high-contrast Bodoni with typewriter details. Centred and quiet; the field of research is the headline.
  • B. TurquoiseDark turquoise green with off-white, Newsreader serif and a plain sans. Left-aligned editorial grid that sends academics, attorneys and organisers straight to Research, Book or Expertise.
  • C. ApparatusA scholarly page: one column of argument with the evidence in the margin. Claims carry note numbers, and the notes are the publications.
  • D. LiteraryModelled on leoniekebaerwaldt.com: your name in thin capitals inks in as you scroll, a light typewriter face for text, sentences set on staggered lines, the book on a grey plinth, a quote carousel, a ruled publications table and a full-screen menu.
  • E. QuestionsOrganised around research questions rather than the person. Bright white, one modern sans, and a highlighter mark on the question you open. Each question expands to a short answer and the papers behind it. Separate routes for students, legal teams and researchers.
  • F. CasebookThe familiar academic-profile layout: identity and contact fixed in an oxblood sidebar, the work scrolling beside it. Working publications filter by type and topic, with a cite and BibTeX button on every item.

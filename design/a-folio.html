The book

Psychopathy Unmasked

The Rise and Fall of a Dangerous Diagnosis

Psychopathy assessments are used across the legal system to inform sentencing, parole and transfer decisions. This book surveys the research and finds that people labelled psychopaths are not meaningfully more dangerous than, or psychologically different from, other offenders. It argues that the legal use of psychopathy assessments should end.

The MIT Press, 2025. 328 pp. ISBN 978-0-262-55220-2

About

Rasmus Rosenberg Larsen is Associate Professor of Forensic Epistemology and Philosophy of Science at the University of Toronto Mississauga, and an Affiliated Scientist at the National Center for Ontological Research.

His research has appeared in The Lancet Psychiatry; Psychology, Public Policy, and Law; and Philosophical Psychology. He holds a PhD in philosophy of science from the University at Buffalo.
